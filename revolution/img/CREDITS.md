# Image credits

List every image added to `img/` here: the file, what it shows, who made it, and where it came from.
Then add it to `js/data/assets.js` so the game uses it.

Only add images we may use: paintings and engravings from before 1900 (public domain), faithful photos of them
(for example from Wikimedia Commons), or pictures you made yourself.

Portraits were cropped to head and shoulders and resized to 256×256. Scenes were resized to 1280 pixels wide.

## Map data

The coastline, islands, lakes, rivers and colony borders come from **Natural Earth** 1:10m data
(naturalearthdata.com), which is in the public domain. `tools/build-geodata.mjs` cuts it to the colonies and writes
`js/data/geodata.js`. Modern reservoirs, canals and borders drawn after 1776 (West Virginia, Washington D.C., the
Canadian provinces) are left out. New York harbor keeps a hand-drawn coast so Manhattan and Staten Island stay islands.
