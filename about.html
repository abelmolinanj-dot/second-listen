About

Everything you missed the first time.

Second Listen turns a class recording into a transcript you can replay from any sentence. Highlight what matters, then play it back as a reel or turn it into flashcards.

It's an independent project made by a student. It isn't affiliated with any school or with the makers of the tools it uses.

How it works

  1. Choose a recording, or paste a transcript you already have.
  2. A speech model runs inside your browser and writes the transcript. The first recording downloads the model, up to about 65 MB.
  3. Replay, highlight and study. Your work is saved in that browser.

Your recordings are never uploaded. Read exactly what stays on your device.

What it costs

Nothing. There are no paid plans, ads or accounts.

What it can't do yet

  • It makes mistakes. The speech model is small enough to run on a phone, so names, technical terms, noisy rooms and overlapping voices can come out wrong. Check anything important against the recording.
  • English is the most tested language. Other languages are available but have had much less testing.
  • Long recordings take time. Your own device does the work, so speed depends on your phone or computer.
  • Your notes live in one browser. They don't sync between devices. Use Back up notes to keep a copy or move them. Backups don't include audio.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. It needs a free GitHub account, and what you write there is public, so please leave out personal details and anything from your recordings.
