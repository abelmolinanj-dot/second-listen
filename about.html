About

Everything you missed the first time.

Second Listen turns a class recording into a transcript you can replay from any sentence, then breaks it down: the key ideas with what was said about each one and every place it comes up, the vocabulary, the people and dates, and an outline of the class in parts. Highlight what matters and play it back as a reel.

It's an independent project made by a student. It isn't affiliated with any school or with the makers of the tools it uses.

How it works

  1. Choose a recording, or paste a transcript you already have.
  2. A speech model runs inside your browser and writes the transcript. The first recording downloads the model, up to about 65 MB.
  3. Tap a sentence to hear it, or hold it to highlight. Open Breakdown for the key ideas and vocabulary, or Outline to see the class in parts. Your work is saved in that browser.

Your recordings are never uploaded. Read exactly what stays on your device.

What it costs

Nothing. There are no paid plans, ads or accounts.

What it can't do yet

  • It makes mistakes. The speech model is small enough to run on a phone, so names, technical terms, noisy rooms and overlapping voices can come out wrong. Check anything important against the recording.
  • The Breakdown is automatic. It quotes your transcript instead of explaining it, and it can miss a term or mislabel a name. People and terms work best in English.
  • English is the most tested language. Other languages are available but have had much less testing.
  • Long recordings take time. Your own device does the work, so a two-hour class can take a long while on a phone. Keep the screen on and plug it in. If it stops, your progress is saved and you can continue from there.
  • Your notes live in one browser. They don't sync between devices. Use Back up notes to keep a copy or move them. Backups don't include audio.

Contact

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on GitHub. It needs a free GitHub account, and what you write there is public, so please leave out personal details and anything from your recordings.

Credits

Speech recognition uses OpenAI Whisper, run through Transformers.js and ONNX Runtime. The Breakdown uses compromise. Long recordings are read with Mediabunny. Vocabulary is told apart from everyday words with a frequency list from FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave. See the full license notices. The source code is public, so you can check how it works.
