Privacy

Last updated November 7, 2026

The short version

Your recordings and notes stay on your device. Second Listen has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no server of its own, so it can't see what you record or write.

What stays on your device

  • Recordings. They're transcribed by a speech model running in your browser and saved in that browser's storage. They're never uploaded.
  • Notes. Transcripts, highlights and settings are saved in that browser's storage.
  • Breakdowns and outlines. Key ideas, vocabulary, people, dates and the outline are worked out in your browser from your transcript.

There's no copy anywhere else, so Second Listen can't recover them for you. If you clear your browser data or lose your device, they're gone unless you made a backup.

What other services can see

Your browser contacts three services to load the app:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site, the speech model files and the word list the Breakdown uses.
  • jsDelivr delivers the speech, language and audio software.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typeface.

As with any website, these services receive technical details such as your IP address and browser type, and they handle those under their own policies. They don't receive your recordings or notes.

Cookies and tracking

Second Listen sets no cookies and includes no tracking or advertising code. It uses browser storage only to save your work and settings on your device.

Backups

Back up notes downloads a file to your device. Second Listen never sees that file. It contains your notes in readable form, so keep it somewhere safe.

Deleting your data

Deleting a note removes it and its audio from that browser. To remove everything, clear this site's data in your browser settings. If you installed the app, uninstall it too.

Other people's voices

Recordings can include other people. You're responsible for getting any permission your school or local law requires. See the Terms.

Children

Second Listen isn't directed at children under 13. It doesn't knowingly collect anyone's personal information, because it doesn't collect your recordings or notes at all.

If this page changes

Any change will appear here with a new date. The source code and its history are public, so anyone can check this page against what the app does.

Contact

Open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please leave out personal details.
